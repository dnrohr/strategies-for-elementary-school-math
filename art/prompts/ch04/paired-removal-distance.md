# CH04 paired spread art brief

Create a two-panel **vector/composite** spread for `72 − 39 = 33`.

Left panel — **removal**: exactly 72 counters arranged as 7 groups of 10 plus 2; a separate tray receives exactly 39 counters (3 groups of 10 plus 9); 33 remain in the source area. Arrows point from source to removed tray. Caption: “Take 39 away from 72.”

Right panel — **distance**: a clean number line begins at 39 and ends at 72. Highlight three forward jumps: +1 (39→40), +30 (40→70), +2 (70→72). A brace over the complete route reads 33. No counters leave a pile. Caption: “Measure the gap from 39 to 72.”

Use distinct arrow direction, labels, and spatial grammar so removal and distance cannot be confused. Raster text is prohibited; all labels must remain editable vector text. Validate 39 + 33 = 72 and 72 − 39 = 33.
