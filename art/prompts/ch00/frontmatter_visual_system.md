# CH00 visual-system brief

## Purpose

Create a reusable opening-spread visual language for the book. This is a layout/art direction brief, not a raster-generation prompt. Any generated organic art must contain no words, numerals, or other rasterized text; add exact labels as vector overlays during layout.

## Opening composition

Show one neutral arithmetic problem at the center of a paper-textured spread, with six thin pathways radiating outward: counted units, a number-line jump, grouped blocks, a hand/finger gesture, written-symbol fragments, and a quiet abstract halo. Do not attach any pathway to a visible identity or imply that one person owns one mode. Use restrained navy, blue, coral, and green accents with black/white structure and non-color cues.

## Mathematical checks

- If `7 + 5` is depicted, show exactly seven plus five units and a resulting group of twelve.
- Number-line arrows must move from 7 through five unit intervals to 12.
- A “make ten” inset must show 7 + 3 + 2, not an unmarked group of ten.
- If hands appear, show five digits per hand and no impossible joints; hands are illustrative only, never evidence of a solver’s identity.
- Labels and equations are vector overlays, never generated inside raster art.

## Accessibility and provenance

Use line style, arrow direction, grouping, and labels in addition to color. Save any final prompt and provenance beside the asset. Review quantity, anatomy, contrast, and whether the image accidentally suggests a different computation.
