# CH01 image review — 7 + 5

Task ID: `QA-IMAGE-REVIEW`
Review date: 2026-09-08
Scope: the opening image and all 14 reader-facing method images, inspected in the built edition; the raster source was also checked at full resolution for hand anatomy.

## Overall opinion

The ten-frame and dot-group methods are the strongest images. The chapter's repeated oversized arrowheads are the main comprehension problem. The finger sequence is not publication-safe: the middle panel's right hand has six visible digits—five raised fingers plus a separate thumb at the inner edge.

## Priority findings

- Replace Method 04 with a deliberately posed or retouched sequence whose fingers separate cleanly in every panel.
- Redraw the arrow system in Methods 01–03, 10, 13, and 14 with small terminal heads that never cover a tick, counter, or equation.
- Keep Methods 05–07 and 11 as visual references for the chapter's clearest explanatory style.
