## Overall opinion

The chapter has several strong place-value views, especially the exchange and verbal panels. Its number-line images are the weakest: arrowheads cover close ticks, and Method 10's +40 and +8 movements visually collide near 77.

## Priority findings

- Redraw Methods 02 and 10; their arrowheads materially interfere with exact landings.
- Simplify transition symbols in Methods 03, 04, 06–09, 13, and 14 as a coordinated style pass.
- Preserve the concrete exchange logic in Methods 07–09.
