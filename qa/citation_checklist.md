# Citation and editorial QA

- [ ] Constructed accounts are unmistakably framed as constructed.
- [ ] Every empirical claim maps to a verified source record.
- [ ] Citation details and quotations were checked against the source.
- [ ] Mixed evidence and inference are labeled.
- [ ] No fixed learning-style, left/right-brain, or diagnostic claims appear.
- [ ] Developmental sequence is presented as approximate, not universal.
