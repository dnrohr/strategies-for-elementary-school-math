# CH11 image review — 27 × 46

Task ID: `QA-IMAGE-REVIEW`
Review date: 2026-09-08
Scope: the opening image and all 18 reader-facing method SVGs, inspected in the built edition.

## Overall opinion

The flagship chapter offers real variety, but some high-density methods do not survive normal reading size. The lattice and 30-row compensation views are the biggest comprehension risks. Area partitions and written partial products are much stronger.

## Priority findings

- Redesign Methods 04 and 14 at a larger explanatory scale.
- Increase the width/legibility of narrow correction regions in the opening and Methods 02, 10, and 15.
- Reduce transition arrows in Methods 01, 05, 07, 12, and 16.
