# CH14 art prompts — `3/4 + 2/3 + 5/12`

Use the book palette: blue, orange, and green for quantities/result, purple for transformation, teal for movement, with patterns and outlines so color is never the sole code. Add numerals and labels as vector/layout overlays, never raster text.

1. **Three equal bars:** quarter, third, and twelfth partitions aligned to one whole, then all redrawn in 12 equal cells with 9 + 8 + 5 marks.
2. **Strategic pairing:** a 12-cell stage (9 + 5 = 14) followed by a 6-cell stage (7 + 4 = 11), preserving whole length.
3. **Number line:** proportional 0–2 line with 12 intervals per whole and jumps of 9, 8, and 5 ending at 22/12.
4. **Improper to mixed:** 22 equal cells shown as one complete 12-cell whole plus 10 cells, then paired into 5 of 6.
5. **Symbolic column:** clean equation cards for 9/12 + 8/12 + 5/12 = 22/12; no rasterized text.

Every prompt must specify equal wholes, exact cell counts, accessible patterns, and a composite/vector overlay pass.
