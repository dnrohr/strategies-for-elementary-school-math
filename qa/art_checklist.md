# Art QA

- [ ] Asset has a saved brief/prompt and provenance note.
- [ ] Organic art has plausible anatomy, exact digits, and consistent left/right orientation.
- [ ] Quantity and gesture match the manuscript step.
- [ ] Mathematical overlays are vector and exact.
- [ ] No generated raster text is used.
- [ ] Contrast, non-color cues, and print resolution pass.
