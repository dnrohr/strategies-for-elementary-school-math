## Review performed

- R02 corrected a scoping-review count and clarified that finger-related fMRI regions were defined using Neurosynth meta-analytic maps rather than participant-specific localizers.

- All 42 DOI URLs resolved to a destination during automated checking; 23 returned content and 19 publisher destinations denied automated access. None returned a missing-link response.

## Editorial guardrails established

- Strategy development is a changing repertoire, not a universal ladder or fixed child type.
- Fast answers and task labels such as “retrieval” do not prove a specific private mechanism.
- Finger use, finger gnosis, motor skill, and finger-related neural activation are different measures.
- Working-memory interference does not establish conscious inner speech or visual imagery.
- Whole-number bias describes several patterns and competing explanations, not one learner trait.
- Number-line and multiple-representation findings remain specific to their tested tasks and intervention packages.
- Conceptual and procedural knowledge can interact; neither is a fixed learner identity.
